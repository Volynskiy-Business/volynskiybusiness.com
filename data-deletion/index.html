Data Deletion Policy

Your Right to Delete Personal Information

Effective Date: July 22, 2025
1

Overview

This page describes how users can request the deletion of their personal data collected through our application PDG Studio AI Messenger, associated with the WhatsApp Business API or Facebook Login.

We respect your right to privacy and provide a straightforward process for requesting the complete deletion of your personal data from our systems.

❦ ❦ ❦
2

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please send an email to our dedicated privacy team:

Email Request Details:

Email address: privacy@volynskiybusiness.com

In your message, please include the following information:

  • Your full name
  • The phone number or Facebook profile associated with your data
  • "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line
  • Any additional information that may help us identify your account
3

Our Data Deletion Process

Upon receiving your data deletion request, we will follow this process:

Step-by-Step Process:

  1. Identity Verification - We will verify your identity to ensure the security of your data
  2. Data Location - We will locate all instances of your personal data in our systems
  3. Complete Deletion - We will delete your data from our servers and third-party services
  4. Confirmation - We will confirm deletion via reply within 7 business days

Please note that some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes as outlined in our Privacy Policy.

4

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request data deletion, the following information will be permanently removed:

  • Personal Information - Name, email, phone number, and any other identifying information
  • Communication History - All WhatsApp messages and interactions
  • Account Data - Your user profile and preferences
  • Usage Data - Analytics and activity logs associated with your account
  • Third-party Integrations - Data shared with or received from integrated services
5

Important Information

Please Note:

  • Data deletion is permanent and irreversible
  • You will lose access to all services associated with your account
  • We may retain certain data as required by law or for legitimate business purposes
  • Backup systems may retain data for up to 30 days before complete deletion

If you have any questions about how your data is stored or processed, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
